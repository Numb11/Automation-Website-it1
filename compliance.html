Compliance

For the person who owns the risk.

The duties sit with the organisation that controls the data. We design the system so the duties that apply to it can be met.

Where it runs
On a computer the client controls, or one machine kept for that client. Nothing is sent to a public model.
Who can see the records
James Limbert, Joseph Lyons, and anyone named on the job. The client can remove access.
The log
Who asked, what was retrieved, and what was shown. It stays on the same machine.
The contract
Once the company exists, we can sign as processor. No one else is in the model path unless named first.
What we will not say
Not that the firm or a system is GDPR or EU AI Act compliant. Neither has a certificate a supplier can award itself, and the duties depend on the system. Not FCA or SRA compliant. Not that the software will not be wrong.
