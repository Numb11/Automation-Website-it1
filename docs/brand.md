# Limbert Lyons — brand

For organisations that cannot put sensitive information into a public tool: legal practices, accountants, clinics, financial firms, and others. Limbert Lyons is a consultancy. The software is designed and built for the client in front of us. It is not one product sold into every sector.

The authority is the method: how the client’s information is prepared, how the software is tied to their own records, how a test is agreed before the build, and how their people can inspect it. Do not invent clients, years in business, headcount, or certificates. A law-firm workflow may be shown as one example of a system. It is not the company.

## Wordmark

In the header, one line: Limbert Lyons, set in Libre Baskerville. No symbol, no lock, no brain.

- Muted `#5c665e` — secondary text

## Type

- Libre Baskerville for the name and headings. The page stays on cream paper.
- Libre Franklin for body text, navigation, and the small lines under headings.
- No monospace. Diagram labels are set in the same serif as the headings.

The opening of each page is a dark panel: a WebGL volume of points that stay inside a box, with annotation lines. That panel is the instrument. The rest of the page is the document.

## Voice

Short sentences. Name the file, the machine, and the person who signs.

Say:

- Data stays on infrastructure the firm controls.
- Records are not used to train a shared model.
- Outputs cite the source.
- A person at the firm approves them.
- The pass bar is written down, and a missed bar means the final milestone is not billed.

Do not say: compliant, certified, guaranteed, or that the model will not be wrong. Do not say Cyber Essentials or ISO 27001 is held. The refusal of those claims stays visible on the offer page.

The company is not incorporated yet. That sentence stays on the site, in the quiet line at the end of the page, not in the mast.

## Pictures

No photographs. No stock library, no server cupboard, no portraits. Diagrams are drawn: a boundary, a kept set, a path inside the box, a rerun. Phosphor green is only for those marks and for the volume. Brass stays on the paper: rules, the header line, small indexes.
