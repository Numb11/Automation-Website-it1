## 1. What this covers

Limbert Lyons processes personal data only to deliver the written engagement: the data boundary review, the corpus work, the local pilot, and any care retainer that follows.

The subject matter is personal data inside the documents the firm chooses to put in scope. The duration is the engagement, plus the deletion period in clause 8. The nature and purpose are retrieval and drafting on infrastructure the firm controls, or on one UK machine dedicated to the firm.

Types of personal data: whatever the firm includes. That may be names, contact details, financial information, and special-category data if the firm puts it in scope. Limbert Lyons does not ask for special-category data beyond what the workflow requires.

Data subjects: the firm's clients, counterparties, and staff, to the extent they appear in the documents in scope.

## 2. Instructions

Limbert Lyons processes personal data only on documented instructions from the firm, including this agreement and the statement of work. If a law requires processing beyond those instructions, Limbert Lyons tells the firm before doing it, unless the law prohibits that notice.

## 3. People

People who can see the data are named in the statement of work. They are bound by confidence. Access is individual, removed when the person stops working on the engagement.

## 4. Security

Limbert Lyons applies the measures in the security note dated with this agreement:

- the model runs on the firm's network, or on one dedicated UK machine
- no prompt or document is sent to a public model provider
- named accounts and multi-factor authentication
- a log of user, time, question, documents retrieved, and draft shown, kept on the same machine
- no copy of the corpus taken off the machine unless the statement of work says so

The firm is responsible for the security of the network it asks Limbert Lyons to use.

## 5. Subprocessors

There are no subprocessors in the model path. The model path is the route from a document and a question to an answer.

Limbert Lyons does not add a subprocessor for the model path without the firm's prior written consent. Any subprocessor for email or file transfer used to run the engagement is listed in Schedule 1 before work starts. Limbert Lyons remains liable to the firm for a subprocessor's processing.

## 6. Assistance

Taking into account the nature of the processing, Limbert Lyons assists the firm with:

- requests from data subjects
- security of processing
- notification of a personal data breach to the ICO and, where required, to data subjects
- data protection impact assessments
- prior consultation with the ICO

Limbert Lyons tells the firm of a personal data breach without undue delay after becoming aware of it. The firm's duty to notify the ICO, where it applies, remains the firm's.

## 7. Audit

Limbert Lyons makes available the information the firm reasonably needs to show that Article 28 is being met, and allows for an audit the firm conducts or mandates, on reasonable notice, during working hours, without disrupting the firm's own systems. A copy of the log export and the security note is the first answer to an audit, not a fishing expedition through unrelated clients. There are no other clients on the machine.

## 8. End of the engagement

At the firm's choice, Limbert Lyons deletes or returns all personal data processed under this agreement, and deletes existing copies, unless the law requires storage. Deletion is confirmed in writing. The firm keeps the corpus and the log on its own machine. Those were never Limbert Lyons' records to keep.

## 9. International transfers

Personal data is not transferred outside the UK. If a transfer ever became necessary, it would not happen without the firm's written instructions and a lawful transfer tool.

## 10. Records and training

Client records are not used to train a model that any other person can use.
