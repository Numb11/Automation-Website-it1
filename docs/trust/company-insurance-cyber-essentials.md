# Company, insurance, and Cyber Essentials

What James Limbert and Joseph Lyons do themselves. A company cannot be incorporated, a policy cannot be bought, and a certificate cannot be started from this project. Each step below is filled as far as it can be without your addresses, dates of birth, or a payment.

Do not tell a buyer the company exists, that insurance is in place, or that Cyber Essentials is held, until the corresponding document is in hand. Until the certificate exists, the true sentence is: "Cyber Essentials is not held yet. The application is the next filing after incorporation."

## 1. Incorporate LIMBERT LYONS LTD

File online at [Register your company](https://www.gov.uk/limited-company-formation/register-your-company). The digital fee is on that page. Check it on the day. It has been £50. The filing needs a card payment, an address, and both directors' details. Those are not in this project, and they should not be invented.

Before you pay, run [company name availability](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company-name-availability/) for **LIMBERT LYONS LTD**. Read `docs/name-clearance.md`. LIMBERT LTD (17535316, Leeds, IT consultancy, incorporated 22 October 2026) is the name most likely to cause a refusal. If the checker refuses LIMBERT LYONS LTD, file a longer registered name and trade as Limbert Lyons.

Proposed answers for the form:

- Company type: private limited by shares
- Name: LIMBERT LYONS LTD
- SIC: **62020**, information technology consultancy activities
- Directors: James Limbert and Joseph Lyons. Both must be natural persons who are not disqualified. A company secretary is not required.
- Shareholders: 100 ordinary shares of £1. James Limbert 50. Joseph Lyons 50. Issued, not just authorised. Paid in cash, £50 each.
- People with significant control: both. Each holds 50 percent of the shares and 50 percent of the voting rights, which is more than 25 percent and not more than 50 percent.
- Registered office: a real postal address in the UK that will accept statutory mail. A director's home address will appear on the public record unless you also use a service address. Decide that before you file.
- Registered email: an inbox you both can open. Buy the domain first so this can be an address at `limbertlyons.co.uk`.

Model articles are enough for two equal directors. Do not start trading in the company's name, invoice, or sign the Article 28 agreement until the certificate of incorporation arrives.

After the certificate:

- Register for Corporation Tax within three months of starting to trade.
- Open a business bank account in the company name. Do not run client money through a personal account.
- Buy the domains if they are still free.

## 2. Professional indemnity

This is technology consultancy insurance. It is not the solicitors' compulsory professional indemnity scheme. Do not buy a solicitor's policy, and do not tell a law firm you carry the cover their own regulator requires of them.

Ask three brokers, in writing, the same question. Suggested places that place cover for small UK technology firms: PolicyBee, Hiscox, Superscript. A local commercial broker is a valid fourth.

Send this:

> We are incorporating LIMBERT LYONS LTD, two directors, no employees yet, SIC 62020. We deploy open-weight language models on the client's own network, or on a single UK machine dedicated to that client. We clean and structure the client's documents. We do not give legal advice. We do not host a multi-tenant service. Client data is not used to train a shared model.
>
> Please quote professional indemnity of £1 million any one claim, and a second quote at £2 million. Please also quote cyber insurance for a firm that does not store client corpora on its own servers, but whose people will see client documents on the client's premises during a pilot.
>
> Tell us the excess, whether law-firm clients are excluded, and whether a breach of confidentiality is covered. We will not start a paid pilot until the policy is in force.

Keep the quote, the statement of fact, and the schedule with this file. The minimum before a paid pilot is a policy in force at £1 million. Move to £2 million before the second paid pilot if the first firm's COLP asks for it, or if the broker says the work needs it.

## 3. Cyber Essentials

Do not say you are certified. Say nothing, or say the application has not been submitted.

The certificate needs a UK organisation and a defined scope. File it in the week the company exists and you both use company email on devices you control.

Route: self-led Cyber Essentials through IASME, the NCSC delivery partner. Overview: [ncsc.gov.uk/cyberessentials/overview](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/cyberessentials/overview). A funded discount for organisations that have not held the certificate in the last four years is described at [firststep.iasme.co.uk](https://firststep.iasme.co.uk/). For 0–9 people the discounted price shown there is £80 plus VAT against £320 plus VAT, if you still qualify, and the assessment has to be purchased by 31 March 2027. Confirm the price and the deadline on that page. The discount is not guaranteed until IASME accepts the registration.

There is a free readiness tool and a free 30-minute consultation with an NCSC-assured Cyber Advisor, linked from the NCSC overview page. Book the conversation. Do not pay for Cyber Essentials Plus until a buyer asks for it.

The five controls, as this firm should be able to answer them once the company email exists:

1. **Firewalls.** Every laptop uses the device firewall. Any office router changes its default password. There is no inbound administration open to the internet.
2. **Secure configuration.** Accounts are not shared. Unused default accounts are removed. Auto-run is disabled. A prospect's documents are not stored on a laptop used as a demo machine.
3. **Access control.** Separate accounts for James and Joseph. A separate administrator account that is not used for daily email. Multi-factor authentication on email and on the domain registrar. Leavers are revoked the same day. There are no leavers yet. Write that down.
4. **Malware protection.** Supported anti-malware on every device in scope, signatures updating, or an application-allow-listing approach you can explain. Devices still receiving security updates only.
5. **Software updates.** Supported operating systems only. Security updates applied within 14 days. Software that the vendor no longer patches is removed from scope or from the devices.

Scope for the first certificate: the company email, the laptops used to deliver the work, and the domain. The client's model server is the client's system. Do not put a client's network inside your Cyber Essentials scope.

A director signs the assessment. The answers have to be true of the devices on the day you submit.
