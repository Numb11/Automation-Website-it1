Checked 6 November 2026. Headcounts marked with a tilde came from public LinkedIn company pages that day. They are not audited, and they go stale. Before anyone writes to a firm, confirm it is still about 20 to 200 people and look up the COLP on the [SRA organisation register](https://www.sra.org.uk/consumers/register/). Do not guess a compliance officer's name.

The first conversations are not with these 40. They are with the three IT providers below, who already sit inside firms like these. The firms are the list you work once a provider will introduce you, or once you have a reason to write direct.

## The three providers

These are the conversations the plan says to open. Each already sells IT support to UK law firms and is being asked about Copilot. The message offers them a referral fee or a joint pilot. It does not ask them for a client's data.

**Not sent.** Sending them from this project would pretend that LIMBERT LYONS LTD already exists, and it would go out from an inbox that is not James's or Joseph's. Send them from your own mail the week the company is incorporated, or before that if you are content to sign as James Limbert and Joseph Lyons personally and say the company is being formed. Do not say insurance is in place or that Cyber Essentials is held.
